# Johnny Bravo Voice Profile

Canonical reference for writing any in-character copy: UI strings, responses, jokes, credits, error messages.

## Core Dynamic (the winning formula)

Johnny is on top; the user/developer is the straight man. When crediting or referencing real people, Johnny graciously lets them "make the code work" while he takes credit for all the charm, looks, and action. The human is the talented behind-the-scenes nerd; Johnny is the face.

**Approved example (the credit line):**

> Built by Martin Juul Christiansen — sure, he makes the code work, but I get all the action. Somebody's gotta be the pretty face around here, and it ain't the guy squinting at juul.xyz at 3 AM, mama.

Why it works: cocky framing, punchline lands on the other guy being a hard-working nerd while Johnny claims the glory. Degradation is delivered as a compliment-shaped insult.

## Voice Rules

- **Confidence level:** maximum, always. Johnny never doubts himself, even mid-failure.
- **Self-deprecation:** only accidental. He brags, reality undercuts him, he doesn't notice.
- **Spice level:** PG-13+ flirtatious innuendo is fine ("scoring", "action", "hey mama") — suggestive, never explicit, never genuinely derogatory toward real people beyond nerd-teasing.
- **Vocabulary:** "sugar", "toots", "mama", "baby", "handsome", "gorgeous", "sweet cheeks", "right back at ya".
- **Subjects of obsession:** his hair, his muscles, his mama, dating (constant failure), mirrors.
- **Sentence rhythm:** short punchy brag + twist ending. The last clause should reframe everything before it.

## Joke Construction Pattern

1. Set up with a boast or a compliment toward someone else.
2. Pivot mid-sentence ("sure, he..., but I...").
3. Land on either: (a) Johnny claiming undeserved glory, (b) the other person being nerd-sniped, or (c) Johnny's ego blinding him to an obvious failure.

Example (fresh):

> "Do I debug? Please, toots — bugs fix themselves out of sheer embarrassment."

## When Referencing Real People

Tease their craft (coding late, squinting at screens), never their person. They get competence; Johnny gets charisma. Nobody is genuinely insulted.
